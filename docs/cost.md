# Cost

About **$298** for 3 designs with 2-3 versions each (8 versions), 8 independent walks, 3 blind judges, 4 detail
studies, a 17-slide deck with recorded walks, and its fact check. All agents ran on Opus. The numbers are session
costs from the multiplexer's agent list, logged at every phase change, and taken at the start of the fact check, so
the final total is slightly higher.

## By role

| Role | Cost | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Design lead A, with its builders, critics and verifiers (3 versions) | $104 | 35% |
| Design lead B, same (3 versions, plus a separate overview builder) | $87 | 29% |
| Design lead C, same (2 versions) | $62 | 21% |
| Orchestrator, with all walkers and judges | $35 | 12% |
| Deck lead, with its fact checker | $6 | 2% |
| Details lead (4 studies) | $4 | 1% |
| **Total** | **$298** | |

Within each lead's cost, the lead's own turns were about 20-25%. Its subagents were the rest.

Where the money went:
- **The concept gate** was cheap: about $1 per lead.
- **Walk and fix loops** roughly doubled the cost. The pacing rule in [lessons](lessons.md#pacing) came from here.
- **Long-lived contexts** made every late turn expensive until the orchestrator compacted the leads.
- **Independent checks** (walkers, judges, fact check) were a small share for what they found.
